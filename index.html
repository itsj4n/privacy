Privacy Policy for DualMod

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the DualMod Discord bot operated for the operator's Discord server, its verification pages, and related features for Roblox verification, moderation, support tickets, reports, and bug reports.

Important: DualMod does not receive your Roblox password or your Discord password during Roblox verification. Sign-in and consent take place through Roblox OAuth.

1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for processing in connection with DualMod is:

DualMod Core
Email: dualmod.support@gmail.com.

2. What Data Does DualMod Process?

2.1 Discord Data

Depending on the feature used, DualMod may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, and channel IDs,
  • Discord usernames, display names, and membership status,
  • roles and permissions where required for moderation or access checks,
  • messages, report text, attachments, and ticket content when you submit them to DualMod or through a ticket,
  • server, channel, and role changes that DualMod records in moderation logs, such as members joining or leaving, roles being changed, and channels being created, updated, or deleted.

2.2 Roblox Verification

If you voluntarily start verification and grant consent through Roblox, DualMod generally receives the following from your Roblox OAuth profile:

  • your Roblox user ID,
  • your Roblox username or display name,
  • the link between your Discord account and your Roblox account.

This data is used to store your verification status, assign the Verified role, and, where technically possible, set your Discord nickname to your Roblox name. As a result, your Roblox name and Verified role may be visible to other members of the relevant Discord server.

2.3 Moderation, Tickets, Reports, and Bug Reports

DualMod provides moderation commands such as /ban, /unban, /timeout, and /purge. The purge command deletes selected recent messages from the Discord channel. For these actions, DualMod may process the affected user's Discord ID, the moderator's Discord ID, the selected action, reason, duration, timestamps, and related server or channel information.

Support tickets, Roblox staff reports, and bug reports may contain the content of your submission, Discord IDs, Roblox identifiers or names, attachments, screenshots, videos, logs, timestamps, and review decisions. These data may be stored or displayed in designated private staff channels.

Report review actions include accepting, rejecting, blacklisting, and deleting a report. The report deletion action sends a deletion request for the related report copies. When a support ticket is closed, DualMod archives a transcript in the ticket log and then deletes the ticket channel. These actions may involve the configured report-processing service described below.

2.4 Blacklists and Temporary Restrictions

The /blacklist command can add or remove blacklist entries for Discord users, Roblox users, or Discord servers. Blacklist information may include the target identifier, reason, moderator information, creation time, and expiration time. Blacklist checks can prevent reports from being submitted.

DualMod can also create a temporary automatic blacklist and an approximately eight-hour moderation restriction when its report protection detects a forbidden link or Discord invite fragment. The restriction and its reason may be recorded in the bot's moderation data and reported to the operator's moderation team.

2.5 Discord Activity and AutoMod Logs

To support moderation on the operator's Discord server, DualMod may log edited messages, deleted messages, bulk message deletions, member joins and leaves, member role or nickname changes, server setting changes, channel changes, and actions from Discord's native AutoMod. Depending on the event, a log may include message content, an edited version of a message, an attachment link, a matched keyword or matched content, the relevant Discord IDs, and the event time.

2.6 Technical Access Data

Technical infrastructure and network services used by DualMod may process technical data such as IP addresses, timestamps, error data, and connection data to provide and secure the verification pages and API endpoints. DualMod's normal bot logic does not create advertising profiles and does not intentionally use tracking pixels or marketing cookies.

2.7 Local Bot Storage

The bot may persist verification records, saved Roblox names, blacklist entries, and moderation-lock information in local bot data files. Temporary report payloads and review decisions may also be held in memory while they are being processed.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

Processing takes place for the following purposes:

  • providing the Discord bot features,
  • voluntary Roblox verification and role management,
  • moderation, abuse prevention, and enforcement of server rules,
  • handling tickets, bug reports, and reports,
  • protecting, troubleshooting, and maintaining the stability of the service.

Depending on the feature, the legal basis may be providing the feature you requested, your consent — particularly for Roblox OAuth — or a legitimate interest in maintaining a safe and functioning Discord server. Where legally required, consent is obtained beforehand.

4. Visibility and Disclosure

Your data is not automatically published publicly on the internet. However, it may be visible:

  • to other server members if your Roblox name is set as your Discord nickname,
  • to moderators or support members in designated Discord channels,
  • to the operator and authorized members of the moderation team who can access the bot's stored data,
  • to technical services needed for Discord communication, Roblox OAuth, callback processing, or the technical processing of reports.

Disclosure takes place only where necessary for the relevant purpose, where you initiated it, or where legally required. Data is not sold for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Providers

DualMod may use third-party services. Their own privacy information also applies to their processing:

  • Discord for server, message, and interaction data,
  • Roblox for sign-in and OAuth verification,
  • a configured technical service for the Roblox OAuth callback, where applicable,
  • a configured technical service for forwarding or processing report events, where applicable.

6. International Data Transfers

Third-party providers may process data outside your country or the European Economic Area. In such cases, the safeguards and legal bases for international data transfers provided by the relevant providers apply.

7. Retention and Deletion

Verification data is stored for as long as the link is needed for the server feature. It may be deleted after you contact us, revoke verification, or submit a valid deletion request, unless a legal obligation or documented security reason requires continued retention.

Ticket, report, bug-report, activity-log, and moderation data is retained only for as long as needed for handling, security, documentation, or legal obligations. Closing a ticket deletes the ticket channel after its transcript has been archived in the ticket log. Data held by Discord or other technical providers may also be subject to their own retention periods.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable legal requirements, you may request access to your stored personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to certain processing. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

For requests, contact dualmod.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

9. Security

DualMod uses access controls, OAuth security mechanisms, and time-limited verification links. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never share passwords, bot tokens, API keys, or other credentials through Discord, tickets, or verification forms.

10. Minors

Discord's and Roblox's age and usage terms, as well as the laws of your country, apply to use of the service. If a parent or legal guardian's consent is required for processing, the relevant feature must not be used without that consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if DualMod, the services it uses, or legal requirements change. The current version will be made available through the public link provided for DualMod.

This is a draft adapted to the DualMod features currently described and does not constitute individual legal advice. Before publishing it, independently verify the controller details, providers, retention periods, and actual data processing.
